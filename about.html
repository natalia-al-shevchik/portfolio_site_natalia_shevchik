About Me

I’m a UX/UI Designer based in Düsseldorf, Germany, focused on creating user-centered, accessible digital products that balance usability and visual clarity. With a background in education and mentoring, I bring strong analytical thinking and a deep understanding of user needs into my design work.

After transitioning into design, I built end-to-end UX/UI projects across e-commerce, wellness, lifestyle, and productivity, applying research, design thinking, prototyping, and iteration to create meaningful and intuitive interfaces.

I further strengthened my profile through a specialization in Frontend Development for Designers, enabling smooth design–development handoffs and responsive, implementation-ready designs. Fluent in English, German, Spanish, French, and Russian, I thrive in international, cross-functional teams.
