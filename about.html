About Me

I'm a UX/UI Designer based in Düsseldorf, creating user-centered, accessible digital products that balance usability and visual clarity. With 20+ years in education and mentoring and a specialization in Frontend Development for Designers, I bridge empathy, design thinking, and implementation bringing that combination to every cross-functional team I join.

My projects span e-commerce, wellness, productivity, and education including a UX/UI Challenge Generator, a design learning tool that reflects both my craft and my passion for helping others grow. Fluent in English, German, Spanish, French, and Russian.
